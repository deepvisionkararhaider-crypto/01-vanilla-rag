# Acme Corp — Remote Work Handbook (Excerpt)

_Fictional, synthetic handbook content used only as demo data._

## Working hours and time zones

Acme is remote-first. Core collaboration hours are **13:00–17:00 UTC**, during
which all teammates are expected to be reachable for meetings and synchronous work.
Outside core hours, employees may flex their schedule to suit their time zone, as
long as their total weekly commitment of **40 hours** is met and deadlines are kept.

## Equipment stipend

Every employee receives a one-time **home office stipend of $1,200** to set up a
productive workspace. The stipend covers a desk, chair, monitor, lighting, and
peripherals. A recurring **$75 per month** internet and utilities allowance is
provided to all fully remote staff.

Equipment purchased with the stipend remains the property of the employee. Repeated
hardware refreshes are eligible every **three years** for laptops and every
**five years** for monitors and furniture.

## Time off

Employees accrue **20 days of paid time off (PTO)** per year, plus company-wide
holidays and a company shutdown between December 24 and January 1. PTO requests of
three or more consecutive days should be submitted at least **two weeks in advance**
through the HR portal. Unused PTO up to five days rolls over into the following
calendar year.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews run on a **semi-annual** cycle, in June and December. Each cycle
includes a self-assessment, peer feedback, and a manager calibration session.
Compensation changes take effect at the start of the following quarter.

## Security expectations

Remote employees must use company-managed devices with full-disk encryption and
multi-factor authentication enabled for all work accounts. Accessing the internal
VPN is required for administrative systems. Security incidents must be reported to
the on-call security team within **one hour** of discovery.
