# Acme Corp — Refund, Support & Warranty Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-15. This is a fictional, synthetic policy used only as demo data._

## Refunds

Refunds are available within **30 days of purchase** for all standard plans. To
request a refund, contact support with your order number and the reason for the
request. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within
**5 to 7 business days**.

Digital add-ons and one-time integrations are refundable within 14 days if they
have not been activated. Usage-based overage charges are non-refundable once the
billing cycle has closed.

## Enterprise terms

Enterprise contracts have custom refund terms defined in the master services
agreement (MSA). Annual enterprise plans may be prorated at Acme's discretion when
a customer downgrades mid-term. Early termination of a multi-year agreement may
incur a fee equal to the remaining committed spend.

## Technical support

Technical support is available **24/7 for enterprise customers** via phone, email,
and a dedicated Slack Connect channel. Standard-plan customers receive email
support with a response target of **one business day**. Priority support, which
guarantees a one-hour first response during business hours, can be added to any
standard plan for an additional monthly fee.

Support severity levels:

- **Sev1** — production down; 1-hour response (enterprise and priority only).
- **Sev2** — major feature broken; 4-hour response.
- **Sev3** — minor issue or question; next business day.

## Warranty

Hardware carries a warranty covering manufacturing defects for **twelve months**
from the date of delivery. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse,
unauthorized repair, accidental damage, or normal wear and tear. Warranty claims
require proof of purchase and a return authorization number issued by support.
